# RF-03 realistic Chinese parcel sorting layout
- Open-top hub: 26.0m x 16.0m x 4.6m with insulated panels, portal steel, cable tray, dock door, electrical cabinets, fire points and safety markings.
- Main line: 19.0m x 1.0m continuous rubber belt at 0.82m working height.
- West start: enclosed automatic singulation/infeed machine visibly discharges parcels through a short roller transfer.
- Process order: automatic infeed, DWS barcode/weight portal, swing-wheel sorter, three north gravity roller chutes and an east drive module.
- Manual exception task: three 1.12m x 0.82m x 1.74m galvanized route cages on the south pedestrian lane.
- Static architecture and machinery are one non-interactive environment GLB. Three parcels, three route cages and the DWS terminal are seven independent interactive GLBs.
