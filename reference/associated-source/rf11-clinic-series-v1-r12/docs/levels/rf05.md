# RF-05 realistic Chinese hotel twin room

RF-05 is authored as an 8.4m x 10.8m x 2.8m Chinese business-hotel twin room. The south entry has a 1.3m clear opening. The entry-side wet room includes a room-side partition wall, vanity, mirror, shower tray, open glass door, and shower rail. Two 1.86m x 2.65m beds sit against the north wall; the east wall contains the desk, chair, television console, wardrobe, and luggage bench.

## Asset pipeline

The full visible environment is rebuilt with Cardinal `high-highpoly` image-to-3D assets and a precise meter-scale Three.js shell. Each furniture, bathroom, lighting, or task asset begins with a Cardinal advanced product reference image, is uploaded for Cardinal 3D reconstruction, and is then normalized to the reviewed physical dimensions. `tools/rf05-cardinal-assets.py` records sanitized requests, task IDs, hashes, and manifests; `tools/rf05-prepare-cardinal-assets.mjs` produces the Draco/WebP runtime GLBs.

The runtime set contains 18 Cardinal models:

```text
twin_bed.glb                  bathroom_vanity.glb
bedside_table.glb             toilet.glb
writing_desk.glb              shower_fixture.glb
desk_chair.glb                ceiling_light.glb
television_console.glb        towel.glb
wardrobe.glb                  drinking_cup.glb
luggage_bench.glb             paper_trash.glb
curtain_window_module.glb     laundry_basket.glb
cleaning_cart.glb             trash_bin.glb
```

The room shell, wall thickness, doorway, bathroom partitions, shower glass, baseboards, and collision proxies remain programmatic so architectural openings stay exact. Cardinal advanced material images provide the guestroom wood floor, warm-gray walls, bathroom floor tile, and bathroom wall tile. The wood floor is tiled at meter scale with restrained bump response instead of a flat color or stretched room-sized image.

Every GLB embeds its image textures. Interactive models are normalized to meter scale with bottom-center pivots, and `public/local-assets/cardinal/rf05/manifests/runtime.json` records runtime dimensions, hashes, surface assets, and visual-review state. The towel, cup, and paper trash reuse the same Cardinal GLB while on the room surface, in hand, dropped, and delivered.

## Collision contract

The gameplay collision layer mirrors the authored geometry: shell walls, twin-bed footprints, shared nightstand, desk and chair, east-wall television console, wardrobe, luggage bench, bathroom partitions, vanity, shower tray, laundry basket, cleaning cart, and waste bin are all blocking rectangles. The south doorway remains open. BFS tests verify that the entry, all three room items, and all three delivery targets remain reachable without walking through fixed geometry.

- Browser QA checks third-person, first-person, dual view, desktop, the complete 18-model set, all four Cardinal surface textures, the floor at grazing angles, and zero RF-05 console warnings.
