## Design Context

### Users
ARC developers, evaluators, and embodied-agent operators inspect this scene in a browser through the same first-person, third-person, and dual-view runtime used by the other levels. The job is to recognize and traverse a faithful reconstruction of the supplied small-office footage.

### Brand Personality
Observed, practical, and lived-in. The scene should feel like a real compact working room captured on a phone: quiet daylight, utilitarian furniture, and light everyday clutter rather than a staged showroom.

### Aesthetic Direction
A realistic daylight office with warm wood underfoot, cool white walls, black structural frames, and sparse red accents. The defining image is the contrast between filtered window light and the warm ceiling cove, anchored by the handwritten mobile whiteboard, red chair, curved display, exposed AC hose, and floral chaise cushion.

### Design Principles
- Preserve the measured room relationships before adding decorative detail.
- Make the five reference anchors recognizable from the default camera.
- Keep materials restrained and slightly imperfect; avoid showroom cleanliness.
- Use procedural geometry and textures so the production build remains self-contained.
- Keep the narrow central route legible for both human and Agent navigation.
