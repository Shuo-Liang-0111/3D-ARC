## Root cause and changes

The v3 engine reproduced the reported failure at player x=0.6, z=-4.2/-4.4/-4.55, yaw=π: the unloaded robot can stand there and detects the parcel, but F returns `blocked_pickup`. The old 2D payload envelope intersects the low conveyor even though the carried parcel should clear its top. The earlier QA station at z=-3.85 did not cover those natural close approach positions.

The shared RF-01 scene now places the parcel 8 cm higher within the existing cargo adapter. The engine derives the carried envelope's lower height from that adapter and the frozen robot grip/tilt. The belt collider declares its actual 0.754 m top. The payload can extend over the low belt while robot body, gantry, cage, wall and doorway collision remains active. Decoded-GLB corner transforms verify more than 1 cm vertical clearance for every parcel in the settled carry pose. Robot models, controls, camera, locomotion and shared envelopes are unchanged.

The line has three 1.4 m modules, totaling 4.2 m, with 1.6 m parcel center spacing and at least 1.05 m box-to-box clearance. A powered 0.7 m/s phase drives the parcels, rubber surface seams and added end-drum witness marks. The phase accumulates continuously so drum rotation does not jump when parcel feeding wraps. Completion/failure freeze and reset behavior remain unchanged.

The small infeed is generated by Cardinal task `aigc_9a3e3bdc951143c785a9da9d6d2cb7fe`, using `high-highpoly`, PBR and GLB. It is uniformly normalized to 1.6 × 0.6706 × 0.4892 m. A 0.2977 m mounting plinth brings the outlet to the 0.7032 m belt surface. The source is preserved under `./authoring/server-production/rf01-sorting-v4/source`; the immutable runtime GLB is `public/local-assets/cardinal/rf01/models/runtime/sorting-v4/compact-infeed.glb`. Original RF-03 assets remain unchanged.

All three RF-01 variants use the same scene and objects. Their existing completion rules and final-objective-only HUD remain unchanged. No floor markings are restored.
