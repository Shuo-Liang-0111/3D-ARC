## Delivered behavior

Book IDs and support heights persist through interactions; ground placement is collision checked. Automatic alignment turning follows the existing free-turn rule. Original RF-02 objectives and rewards are preserved.

The website movement outage was traced to 12 abandoned simulation connections exhausting the backend capacity. The gateway was restarted to release them. `server/mujoco-bridge.mjs` now pings clients every 15 seconds and terminates unresponsive connections and their simulation peers, bounds pending commands, applies an upstream handshake timeout and preserves retryable capacity rejection codes. Robot models, controls and kinematics were not changed.
